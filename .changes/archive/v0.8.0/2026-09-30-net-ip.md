NEW: net_ip — first IP-address library: IPv4 and IPv6 addresses as value types. One family-tagged `IpAddress` over two concrete structs (`Ipv4Address` on UInt32, `Ipv6Address` on UInt128); strict numeric parsing (`parse`/`parse_ipv4`/`parse_ipv6` plus non-throwing `try_parse`/`is_valid`) with a typed `IpParseError` carrying a kind and byte position; RFC-named classification predicates (unspecified, loopback, private, link-local, multicast, broadcast, IPv4-mapped); explicit mapped handling (`is_ipv4_mapped`, `to_ipv4_mapped`, `to_ipv4`, `unmap`); `format` with RFC 5952 canonical IPv6 output; and `next`/`prev` returning Optional at the range ends. No socket and no FFI: pure value library, leaf dependency.
